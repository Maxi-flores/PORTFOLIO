## Architecture → Funnel Engineering (Operational Analogy)

- Treated funnel design like structural engineering: every layer must carry load, stay legible, and connect to the next constraint without collapse.
- Applied architectural discipline to digital systems thinking:
  - **Foundations** → measurement and tracking fundamentals (you can’t optimize what you can’t measure).
  - **Spatial hierarchy** → information hierarchy and UX flow (what the user sees first determines the path).
  - **Load paths** → conversion paths (every step introduces friction; friction needs engineering).
  - **Structural grids** → layout systems for scalable creative (consistent spacing, legibility, and responsiveness).
