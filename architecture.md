# Portfolio Architecture: Ad Ecosystem & Systems Alignment

## 1. Executive Summary & Goal
This repository (wai.powerframe.online) serves as an interactive, data-driven portfolio designed to showcase **B2B Lead Generation & Cross-Platform Ad Arbitrage (Meta/Google Ads Ecosystem)** alongside systems development (cloud + automation). 
The architecture bridges a diverse background (Architecture, Meta Ads, Cloud Data, Bilingual Customer Support) into repeatable competencies that support lead qualification, technical audits, and scalable delivery.

---

## 2. Core Themes & Google Competencies
Every project and period must map to at least two of these core competencies:
*   **[COMP-1] Lead Identification & Prospecting:** Sourcing high-value advertisers using digital signals.
*   **[COMP-2] Market Analysis & Trend Forecasting:** Identifying growth industries and regional shift signals.
*   **[COMP-3] Technical Literacy & Data Quality:** Leveraging cloud, data fundamentals, and ad infrastructure.
*   **[COMP-4] Consultative & Commercial Mindset:** Translating business goals into advertising solutions (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube, App).
*   **[COMP-5] Cultural & Linguistic Agility:** Native fluency in Dutch/Spanish, navigating NL, ES, and PT markets.

## 5. GitHub Copilot Instructions & Prompt Engineering Rules
When generating text or code for this repository, Copilot must adhere to the following rules:
1.  **Action-Oriented Language:** Use strong, metrics-driven verbs (e.g., *Analyzed, Built, Qualified, Leveraged*). Avoid passive text.
2.  **Explicit Mapping:** Every project page must include an interactive or visual metadata block explicitly stating: `Mapped Google Competency: COMP-X` and `Answers Interview Question: Y`.
3.  **Tone:** Professional, analytical, growth-oriented, and subtly tailored to Google’s internal culture (Googley, data-first, collaborative).
4.  **No Feigned Experience:** Do not invent metrics; focus on methodology, tech stacks used, and transferable operational frameworks.
