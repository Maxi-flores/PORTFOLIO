# Portfolio Architecture: Ad Ecosystem & Systems Alignment

## 1. Executive Summary & Goal
This repository (wai.powerframe.online) serves as an interactive, data-driven portfolio designed to showcase **B2B Lead Generation & Cross-Platform Ad Arbitrage (Meta + Intent-Based Search Networks)** alongside systems development (cloud + automation). 
The architecture bridges a diverse background (Architecture, Meta Ads, Cloud Data, Bilingual Customer Support) into repeatable competencies that support lead qualification, technical audits, and scalable delivery.

---

## 2. Core Themes & Core Competencies
Every project and period must map to at least two of these core competencies:
*   **[B2B-Lead-Gen] B2B Lead Generation:** Signal-based lead discovery, qualification, and pipeline execution.
*   **[Cloud-Data] Cloud & Data:** Data modeling, analytics foundations, and cloud-native ops for measurement-ready systems.
*   **[Automation] Automation:** Repeatable diagnostics, connector logic, and workflow automation.
*   **[Cross-Platform-Ads] Cross-Platform Ads:** Performance marketing systems across paid media surfaces with structured experimentation.
*   **[Localization] Localization:** Cross-market messaging, language precision, and localized UX heuristics.
*   **[Systems-Dev] Systems Development:** Product engineering mindset: architecture, tooling, and maintainable delivery.
